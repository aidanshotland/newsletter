### 🛑 Learning to Solve Hard Problems in RL for LLMs by Never Giving Up
Research proposes "Never Give Up" (NGU) as a solution to the "Matthew Effect" in Reinforcement Learning (RL) for LLMs, where standard RL methods disproportionately improve performance on already easier problems. NGU counters this by continuing to sample and focus on hard examples, leading to more balanced and robust model improvements. Experimental results indicate that this strategy significantly enhances LLM performance on tasks that are challenging for baseline models.
