### 🧠 How LLMs Actually Work
Large language models (LLMs) fundamentally operate by transforming text into numerical vector embeddings that capture semantic meaning. They utilize transformer blocks, positional encoding, and attention mechanisms to process and understand sequential data, allowing tokens to weigh the importance of others. The core differences in modern LLMs stem from training data, scale, configuration choices, and post-training optimization.
